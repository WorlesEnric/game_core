## Limits and outstanding acceptance

- **TEST-008 50 × 500 Pass in the existing .NET derivation suite:** `IncrementalAgreementTests` runs 50 narrative seeds × 500 and 50 card seeds × 500 against the oracle; both tests passed in the 114-test derivation assembly. GC-023's additional *player* sweep ran 12 × 32 operations with a reduced injected-divergence witness. The separate 1,000-scope/10,000-target diagnostic workload of 08 was **NotRun**; the 10,000-step integer replay has 10 targets. No 30-second warmup/five independent 120-second timing runs, 1,000 lifecycle cycles, 1,000 vs 10,000 inactive-target scaling comparison, or measured release overhead were performed. Thus `~zero cost` here means removed counting call sites and zero live samples, **not** a measured timing/allocation result.
- The replay runner's worker lanes are a deterministic scheduler model over pure managed derivation; setting `JobsUtility.JobWorkerCount` in Unity does **not** make its integer stage execute as parallel Unity jobs. This run proves equality under four real Unity worker settings **and** simulated producer/completion permutations, not that real concurrent producer jobs were raced. Native physics was not claimed deterministic.
- GC-023 scenario asserts zero control work for 64 idle frames and the pure replay test checks carried unchanged steps; it does not independently assert a live Unity world's 10,000 unchanged *active* simulation steps with per-step control counters. This specific TEST-023 workload remains **NotRun**, not Pass.
- The counter schema has 29 counters (`TelemetrySchema.CounterCount=29`); the player reports the 08-listed families, but this run does not establish allocation-free steady-state performance. No suites are currently failing; the above acceptance items need distinct workloads before being claimed complete.
